NFX test player

Give a manifest address and a relay to resolve the signed manifest over Nostr and find an origin in seeders' beacons, or give an origin and a root directly. Every file is checked against its sha256 and the hash list before the player uses it (NFX-05 §4). By address, the player also joins the browser mesh through any tracker a seeder's beacon names: it then shares verified segments with other viewers, and the trackers and those peers see your IP address.

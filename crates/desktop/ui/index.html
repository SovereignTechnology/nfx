This app keeps and seeds what you watch

A video you watch for a minute or more is kept on this computer and seeded to other viewers while the app runs, so that more people can watch it. While it seeds, this computer's public IP address and the list of videos it seeds are public, signed with this node's key. No address on your home network is announced, and your router is never asked to open a port.

You can turn it off at any time with "Seed videos I watch" below. Videos already kept then stay on this computer until you remove them from History. Nothing is kept until you answer.

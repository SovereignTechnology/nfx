# Twenty-ninth independent audit: M2.0 (`3d4e0d7`, 2026-09-29)

- **Auditors:** four fresh auditors, one per lens, working in parallel from their own clones
  at `3d4e0d7`, with no author review to read:
  - reads: the seeder's own reads, the account's turn and deadlines (9 findings);
  - watcher: the watcher, §4 and the browser client (9);
  - teeth: the suite's teeth and the mock's fidelity (4);
  - lock: the lock, CI and the pay/1 wire (4).

  Each finding then went to a separate adversarial verifier. All 26 were confirmed real and
  reproduced; none was refuted or re-rated.
- **Scope:** branch `m2/contracts` at `3d4e0d7`; the twenty-eighth rework examined hardest.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready to push. 26 findings: 21 Low and 5 Info, none Medium or above. No
  money bound is broken in the honest mock.

**How this round was resolved.** Most findings of the last rounds were one instance of a
class: a rule pinned for some paths or checks but not for their neighbours, which the next
audit then found. So each finding here is resolved for its class, not for the instance
found. Where the suite runs the class, that is as tables of cases over its members, with
planted defects registered against them; not every member has a case, nor every case a
planted defect (see "Left open"). Where the resolution is spec and CI text (findings 13 to
15, 18 and 23 to 26), no defect is planted: the lock's part is run as tables of rows
through the lock, and finding 14 is text only. The classes:

- the seeder, as a matrix of every way a payment or a hello reaches its answer against
  every check it runs, with what it leaves, whom it wakes and the reads' floor;
- the watcher, as every input it can be given against every state a payment can be in;
- §4 and CI, sentence by sentence and tree by tree.

Groups a (the seeder: findings 1 to 9), b (the watcher: 10 to 12, 14, 16, 17 and 19) and c
(origins, the browser client, the lock and CI: 13, 15, 18 and 23 to 26) were made on
branches of their own (`m2/r29-a`, `m2/r29-b`, `m2/r29-c`), each reviewed independently
and corrected, then merged. Group d (how the suite waits: 20 to 22) was made on the merge:
its first draft (`m2/r29-d`, never built) was finished on `m2/audit-loop`, reviewed
independently and corrected there.

After group d, with the lock re-pinned (`c7c3f8e`), four independent reviewers went over
the round:
- one reviewed group d's final diff: 9 issues (2 Medium, 5 Low, 2 Info), and nine
  sentences of this record false or overstated;
- three went through every class table for a member it lacked: 17 gaps in the seeder's
  (rows 1 to 9) and 15 in the watcher's (rows 10 to 12, 14, 16, 17 and 19), each Low or
  Info, and 15 in the rest (rows 13, 15, 18 and 20 to 26: 5 Low, 10 Info).

The two Medium issues are of the suite, not of a money bound: it failed an honest engine
whose entries on threads await tasks of its runtime, and it passed an engine that wakes an
entry before it settles what the wake announces. No money bound is broken in the honest
mock. The findings were resolved in four groups, each on a branch of its own from
`c7c3f8e`: the seeder's tables; the watcher's and the dropped video's; the lock and the
spec text; and how the suite waits. The four were then brought together on one tree. There
the last group's machinery (entries on tasks of the runtime, the helper that closes a
connection, the rule for entries on threads) was applied over the other groups' new cases:
by a scripted rewrite of the call sites, three cases converted by hand, and its wake cases
extended to three kinds of answer they had not run on its own branch (a mint's refusal of
the keys asked for, which the mock code of the seeder's group brought; a second quoted
mint's answers and a keyset listing a payment did not ask for, awaited at step 3, both of
which the mock already gave at `c7c3f8e`). That tree was then read once more, by reviewers
who changed nothing and ran nothing: 31 issues were confirmed and are corrected on it
("The review of the merged tree", below). Before the push a security-focused review went
over everything since `c7c3f8e`: four findings were confirmed, none above Low, and the
three that concern the tree are resolved on it ("The review before the push", below). That
makes the final tree, `9cb71e1`. The suite grew from 66 scenarios to 101, and from 2,092
planted entries to 2,906 (714 defects of the seeder to 882, and 370 of the watcher to
461). The resolutions below are of the final tree. What the three reviews found is in the
sections after the table.

**Decisions taken in the completeness review:**

  reclaimed.* §3a said only that the watcher stops, and the mock dropped the payment: it
  was neither reclaimed, nor awaiting a quote, nor in the ledger, while the seeder could

  `Viewer::ack` does not reach the mint: the calls that finish an incomplete reclaim run
  it.

  stands, and the mock changed. It kept the licence until a payment was made, so the
  licence outlived a session, and a `due` that paid nothing. The licence is now the next
  `due`'s on that ledger: it ends there, paid or not, and at the session's end, and
  `last_pay` neither uses nor ends it (the contract said so with the decision; §3a says so
  since the review of the merged tree). Credit stays within half a window under either
  reading. With the strict one alone the honest mock passed the suite as it stood (run
  37290849134). Three older defects that grant a licence on a refused quote keep it across
  a session's end in the mock, so that they stay the defects they were.

  text did not say when, and the mock judged so. One it answers stays answered whatever
  its reads learn. A peer whose first payment there is still in progress has no account
  yet, and is refused `unknown-video` until it lands.

  as the watcher's identity toward the seeder, and a page takes an identity of its own. So
  a seeder that refuses everything takes half a ceiling for each video under each
  identity, as the one-payment bound already said of identities. Text only.
- *The Harness's seeder serves three videos.* A ledger made since a payment's session
  closed needs a video, and a session, of its own. A real engine's harness must follow.
  The earlier scenarios use two.

  could not be reached: the mock mint's listing only grew. `Harness::refuse_keys` makes a
  mint refuse the keys of a keyset it lists, as one does that lost them, or whose listing
  a cache in front of it outlived. It is a call of the Harness, not a shape of token, so
  that "keeps nothing of that answer" can be pinned.

  had no teeth: a defect only a seeder that asks at its start can show passed. A flawed
  one is registered under a letter of its own, `l`.

  cargo reads TOML, where an escape spells the same name. It now runs the lock where a
  commit in HEAD's history changed a file of the lock or a money path, or where a Cargo
  manifest or Cargo.lock, decoded, names a money crate, as well as where the tree holds a
  money path, a file of the lock, or a money crate's name in the bytes of a Rust file, a
  manifest or Cargo.lock; a manifest or Cargo.lock it cannot read counts. History is
  tested by paths, not by a named commit, so it holds after a squash or a rewritten
  history.

  a test file added to a money crate pass the lock unrun, which is how `listings_at_start`
  went uncounted before, and a count taken from the file it checks matched whatever ran.
  Every test target is run, unit tests and doctests included, with none ignored or
  filtered out.

  as a base URL. No behaviour changes: NFX-03 §4 already served `GET <base>/<sha256>`, and
  the M1 player already fetched under a beacon's base path. Each change is in its spec's
  changelog.
- *An engine's entry futures are `Send`, and its engine is a handle.* The suite runs
  entries on tasks of a runtime, so `hello`, `pay` and `sweep` return `Send` futures and

  wait, and an engine that is no handle to its seeder (one is wrapped in an `Arc`).

  given was idle, unpolled turns alone. It could not hold alone: the honest mock waits for

  never stands idle. One that has been polled 100 times more with nothing under way waits

  harness's thread would leave a check of the suite's to fail in its stead.

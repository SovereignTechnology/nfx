# Twenty-first independent audit: M2.0 (`bf3f6b8`, 2026-09-25)

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `5d16d80..bf3f6b8`, branch `m2/contracts`. The twentieth rework was examined
  hardest, against CDK 0.18.1: its swap's check order, `is_expired`, the signatory's
  keyset handling at startup, and the CDK wallet's proof selection.
- **Method:**
  - 13 new mutants (10 seeder, 3 viewer), a no-op control, 2 fix probes and 11 existing
    flaws as controls, each run against all 62 scenarios on both harnesses: 4 caught, 9
    survived, 7 of them against the spec, with 28 paired repro tests;
  - 600,000 differential parser cases over 4 new seeds, and 38 targeted cases, with a
    new family (exponent and sign spellings, mixed nesting at the depth edge, every C0
    control and DEL, token alphabet edges, escaped keys, 32767- and 32768-byte lines,
    escaped 64-character codes, surrogate-pair duplicate keys): 0 disagreements, 0
    round-trip failures, 0 writer refusals. It also found that the twentieth auditor's
    generator never reached its families 16 to 18, and made them live;
  - the lock job simulated on a clone (passed in 56 s), and the check job with every
    variable `gitlab-ci.yml` sets (passed in 7 min 37 s);
  - 2 new lock routes: a failing re-pinned change committed with a stale force-added
    `crates/target`, and `crates` replaced by a tracked symlink. Both refused.
- **Controls:** adversary 62/62, round_trip 62/62, mutants 311/311 and pay1 2/2.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready to push. 7 findings: 5 Low and 2 Info; no High or Medium. The
  twentieth audit's 7 resolutions held as implemented. The one thing it listed as found
  while resolving was false, and the payment side of the read rules had gone unpinned
  on its strength.
- **Not findings:**
  - two equivalent or harmless mutants: a wait running through the deadline's own
    second (a payment cannot be in such a wait then), and a keyset margin measured from
    arrival (at most 60 s off a margin of twice `account_ttl`);
  - the trade-offs NFX-07 states, among them the two-a-second bound counted per
    calendar second, a payment refused `mint-unavailable` for want of a read, the expiry
    concessions, and a reclaim held incomplete while the mint keeps an expired active
    keyset active;
  - two mock artefacts (waiting entries busy-poll; earlier proofs belong to an older
    keyset under `expire_active_keyset`).

The resolutions are in `1616172`.

**What a read covers.** Each read now records the swaps it covered, fixed when it is
sent. It serves an entry only if it covered every swap undecided for the account as that
entry comes, so a read sent before a swap became unknown serves no entry after it. In
round trips the mock now makes a read's requests when it is sent, and applies what they
found when it is back (`Inner::learn_read`, `Inner::learn_apply`). A read dropped
mid-way has therefore reached the mint, and `state_reads` counts it.

**Expiry, per proof.** A watcher's reclaim refused 12003 with its proofs unspent is
decided per proof. Proofs whose keyset's listed `final_expiry` has passed by the watcher's
clock are lost to the expiry; the rest are taken back in a reclaim of their own
(`Harness::expire_keyset_of` makes such a token).

**Found while resolving:** with reads that serve only what they covered, a peer sharing
one read record across its videos (`ReuseByPeer`) no longer showed in results, but it
still shared the per-second budget across videos. NFX-07 §3 now says reads count
against their own account alone. `bans_expire_and_state_stays_bounded` pins it: a
video's two reads in a second do not hold up the peer's other video.

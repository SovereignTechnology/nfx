# Third independent audit: M2.0 (`62de4ad`, 2026-09-24)

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `5d16d80..62de4ad`, branch `m2/contracts`. That is M2.0 and both reworks
  ([first audit](2026-09-24-m2.0-independent-audit.md),
  [second audit](2026-09-24-m2.0-second-audit.md)).
- **Method:**
  - 22 new mutants of the mock (17 plausible survivors);
  - exploit tests E1 to E20 measuring impact;
  - 30,311 parser probes against the Python reference;
  - lock bypasses run in a clone. None was stopped this time.
- **Controls:** 35/35 scenarios and 61/61 planted flaws passed on the pristine tree,
  and with the auditor's files added.
- **Verdict: not complete for push.**
  - **Suite:** 17 plausible mutants passed all 35 scenarios, with measured impact. The
    worst:
    - 18,000 unpaid chunks in one `debt_ttl` at a cap of 10;
    - 1 sat of credit covering 5,000 chunks of another video.
  - **The honest reference failed its own spec three times:**
    - an unbounded drain through `mint-unavailable`;
    - an honest peer banned when its connection dropped mid-swap;
    - an overpayment when an ack overtook a refusal.
  - **Design:** the global cap was a free, renewable lock-out of paying watchers.
  - **Lock:** six new bypasses were run with both stages green.

The resolutions are in the commit that follows this record. Every surviving mutant is
now a planted flaw, and every exploit a scenario.

| # | Sev | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | Debt was never checked before `debt_ttl`: ageing after 1 s passed (E1: 18,000 chunks at cap 10). | `debt_counts_for_exactly_debt_ttl` checks one second before and at `debt_ttl`. Durations are `Duration`. Flaw `AgeTtlOneSecond`. |
| 2 | High | Cross-video credit was untested: 1 sat on video 1 covered 5,000 chunks of video 0. | `credit_covers_only_its_own_video`. NFX-07 §3 says credit covers only its own video. Flaw `CoveredPeerWide`. |
| 3 | High | **An unbounded drain through `mint-unavailable`**, in the honest viewer and in the spec. A seeder could swap, answer `mint-unavailable`, and be paid again, 20 times over (E13). | NFX-07 §3a: whatever the code, a reclaim that finds a proof spent loses that payment, and the watcher stops. Nothing more is paid until a reclaim completes. §4 applies the same rule after `503`. The mock's reclaim honours outages. Scenario `a_lying_seeder_takes_at_most_one_payment`; flaw `IgnoresReclaimOutcome`. |
| 4 | High | **Lock:** a pipeline variable could empty `CI` and unlock. | The pinned `gitlab-ci.yml` runs `CI=true LOCKED_DIRS_UNLOCKED= … check.sh`, and the check also recognises `GITLAB_CI` and `CI_JOB_ID`. Attacks with either of them set were refused. |
| 5 | High | **Lock:** a target runner (from the environment or an untracked `.cargo/config.toml`) made `cargo test` pass with nothing run. | A `CARGO_*`/`RUST*` allow-list. Cargo configs and toolchain files are refused anywhere cargo reads them: `crates/` and every directory above it, tracked or not. `--compiled` requires the adversary, mutant and pay/1 test binaries each to report exactly the number of tests their pinned sources declare. A runner reached past the environment check fails there ("43 declared, None ran"). |
| 6 | High | **Lock:** nfx-proto's parser could be swapped through its unpinned `lib.rs`, and the `#[path]`/`include!` regex had four bypasses. | nfx-proto's `lib.rs`, `error.rs` and `Cargo.toml` are pinned. The regex is gone. `--compiled` now checks the dep-info of **every** workspace target in the build CI runs: every compiled file must be tracked, and a library or binary may read only its own crate. Every spelling of `#[path]` and `include!` is caught by what the compiler read (four tested), a tracked file from another crate included. |
| 7 | Med | Debt could be freed twice, or never (three mutants; E2 to E4). | The global count is a set of (account, chunk), so a double free is impossible by construction. `debt_is_freed_exactly_once` covers paid-then-aged, aged-then-paid, the wrapping jam and covered chunks. | 8 | Med | **An honest peer was banned when its connection dropped mid-swap:** the viewer reclaimed at once, while the seeder's swap was in flight (E14). | NFX-07 §3a: wait 120 s from sending, even across a dropped connection. Scenario `an_honest_pair_survives_a_dropped_connection`; flaws `EndReclaimsNow`, `EndForgetsPending`, `TimeoutEarly`. |
| 9 | Med | `pay` was not cancel-safe: claimed but uncredited (E7). | The contract makes `pay` cancel-safe. The mock's swap completion credits and holds the account's turn whatever happens to the future. `hold_swap_responses` models "processed, answer held". Scenario `a_dropped_pay_is_credited_or_never_claimed`; flaw `ClaimThenAwaitCredit`. |
| 10 | Med | §3 (forgetting accounts) contradicted §3a (quote equals ledger), and a resync-down viewer paid twice. | Only never-paid accounts may be forgotten; accounts are created by admission or payment, not by `hello`. A quote below the ledger is refused, never resynced. Flaw `ResyncsDown`, with resumed viewers in the quote scenario. |
| 11 | Med | **The global cap locked out every paying watcher**, for free and renewably (E16: 0 of 180). | A full cap refuses unpaid service, never paid service. | 12 | Med | Price checks ran on the first quote only, and always with `max_price == price`. | A resumed quote over the cap is refused, and the honest pair runs with `max_price` = 3 × price. Flaws `SkipsCapOnResume`, `PaysAtCap`. |

A tampered archive is refused. |

| 16 | Med | The parser's own tests, its vectors and the reference reader were unpinned. | The reader moved to `spec/test-vectors/pay1.py` and the tests to `nfx-proto/tests/pay1.rs`. Both are pinned, with `pay1.json`. |

| 18 | Low | The honest viewer overpaid when an ack overtook a refusal (E15). | A refused request is always un-owed; a paid one becomes credit. Scenario `a_viewer_owes_nothing_for_refused_requests`; flaw `RefusedNoCredit`. |

## The rework, verified (author, before the fourth audit)

- **Suite:** 43 scenarios pass against the honest mock.
- **Teeth:** 83 planted defects are each caught, every surviving mutant from all three
  audits among them.
- **Parser:**
  - `pay1.json` holds 20 valid lines, 3 loopback-only and 83 invalid, each verdict
    checked by `pay1.py` in both modes;
  - the Rust reader agrees with the reference on 20,141 cases: the second audit's 141
    probes and 20,000 lines fuzzed from the vectors;
  - it accepts 1,899 of them, and every accepted message survives its writer.
- **Lock:** 37 bypass attempts were refused, the six new ones from this audit included,
  plus a tracked file from another crate included under a renamed `include!`. The last
  four compiled-stage cases were confirmed to fail for their intended reason.
- **CI:** `crates/ci/check.sh` passes in full, with the lock at its start, before the
  build (`--sources`) and at the end (`--compiled`).

# Nineteenth independent audit: M2.0 (`6162815`, 2026-09-25)

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `81a692e..6162815`, branch `m2/contracts`. The eighteenth rework was
  examined hardest, against CDK 0.18.1: its restore filter, its order of checks,
  `blind_sign`, `check_state`, the swap saga's compensation and recovery, and how
  `final_expiry` is set.
- **Method:**
  - 18 new mutants (16 seeder, 2 viewer), a no-op control, 5 known flaws as controls
    and 2 reachability probes, each run against all 62 scenarios on both harnesses: 8
    caught, 10 survived, 5 of them against the spec, each with a paired repro;
  - a CDK-faithful opt-in for the watcher's reclaim restores;
  - 600,000 differential parser cases over 4 new seeds, with a new family (every
    required field at every type and boundary, exotic whitespace between tokens, deep
    nesting in `mints`, 15 to 17 mints, non-object top-level values): 0 disagreements,
    0 round-trip failures, 0 writer refusals;
  - the lock job simulated on a clone (passed in 65 s), and the check job with every
    variable `gitlab-ci.yml` sets (passed in 8 min 20 s);
  - 6 new lock routes, among them moving the `r(…)` mutant, hiding it from the count,
    CRLF line ends through `.gitattributes`, and tokio's `test-util` through
    `[workspace.dependencies]`: all refused.
- **Controls:** adversary 62/62, round_trip 62/62, mutants 298/298 and pay1 2/2.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready to push. 8 findings: 1 Medium, 6 Low and 1 Info. The worst was
  the eighteenth rework's main rule. Once a swap's outputs' keyset had expired, "an input
  spent is the claim" also credited payments the payer had taken back, or spent
  elsewhere while the seeder could not read. A 32-chunk pre-payment, double-spent, was
  credited in full, and 36 chunks were served where the bound is 4. It reproduced on both
  harnesses, once with no read ever failing.
- **Not findings:** the trade-offs NFX-07 or the lock's header states; five equivalent or
  unreachable mutants. Among them, "pending with expired outputs is nothing" (a reserved
  request whose outputs expired cannot sign), and four of `Inner::by_inputs`, which no
  honest path reaches.

The resolutions are in `c6fd650`.

**When expiry erases the evidence, each side keeps its own bound.** The eighteenth
rework's inputs-only rule is withdrawn: nothing can tell a claim from the payer's own
reclaim or a double spend once the outputs' keyset has expired. NFX-07 §3 now states the
concession. A claim not learnt before its outputs' keyset expires reads as unsigned, and
so, with its inputs spent, as nothing:
- the seeder never extends credit it cannot prove;
- its expired outputs are worth nothing to it either;
- the payer's watcher, finding the proofs spent, stops paying that seeder.

§3a states the watcher's counterpart. A reclaim whose outputs expire before it can
restore them is treated as found spent. The seeder's outputs' keyset rule (twice
`account_ttl`) keeps its case to a swap undecided that long. The rule is now pinned, and
it no longer consults the mint's own state for any decision (finding 8).

**Found while resolving:**
- `RetryExpiredStaysUnknown` had been caught only by the withdrawn rule's case. It is now
  caught by its own, the payer's keyset expiring after the first attempt went through:
  a restore still shows the claim, acknowledged in time.
- The mint's own keyset expiring under a reserved request: that request cannot sign.
  Without the withdrawn rule its swap stays undecided until the ordinary completion at
  `account_ttl`, which settles it as nothing; the account then pays again. The scenario
  pins that path.
- A planted "waits past its deadline" proved equivalent: a waiter looks again at every
  poll, and its reuse ends with its second, before any deadline. It was replaced by the
  eighteenth's wait (`WaitsWhileReading`), which does hang.
- NFX-07 had briefly given the watcher a keyset rule for reclaim outputs; it cannot
  refuse to reclaim, so only the concession stands.

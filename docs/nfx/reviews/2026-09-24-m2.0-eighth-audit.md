# Eighth independent audit: M2.0 (`361b856`, 2026-09-24)

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `5d16d80..361b856`, branch `m2/contracts`. The seventh rework was examined
  hardest.
- **Method:**
  - 12 new mutants of the mock: 3 were caught, 7 passed all 62 scenarios, and 2 are
    equivalent. Each surviving mutant came with a paired test that passes against the
    honest mock and fails under the mutant;
  - about 120,000 differential parser cases and an edge battery (depth, line length,
    number bounds, duplicate keys, lone surrogates, IPv6 hosts, ports, `window` 64 and
    65): 0 disagreements;
  - the lock's modes, run in a disposable clone, with the routes of earlier audits.
- **Controls:** 62/62 scenarios, 174/174 planted flaws and pay1 2/2 passed on the pristine
  tree. The CI lock job passed on the pristine clone.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not complete for push. 8 findings: 4 Medium and 4 Low. Seven are gaps in
  the suite's teeth, and one is the lock's exemption for nfx-proto's tests being wider
  than its header says. The parser and the rest of the lock were found sound.
- **Not findings:** the designed trade-offs NFX-07 states, and two equivalent mutants:
  - trimming the debt log at `account_ttl` rather than `debt_ttl` keeps state bounded,
    since the global cap still ages at `debt_ttl`;
  - a watcher ceiling above 64 cannot be reached, because the wire refuses `window` 65.

The resolutions are in `ced7a28`.

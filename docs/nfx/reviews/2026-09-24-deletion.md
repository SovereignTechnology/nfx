# Review: NFX deletions (2026-09-24)

Scope:
- `nfx-proto::deletion`, with `KIND_DELETION` and `deletion.json`;
- the scoped relay's admission (kind 5, applying deletions, refusing replays);
- `Relays::manifests` and `Relays::deleted`, and `sign_deletion`;
- `nfxd delete` and the seeder's `Deleted` state;
- `nfx-wasm` `verifyDeletion` and the player's check;
- NFX-02 §§2 and 6, NFX-04 §§1–3.

Method: `differential-review` (a new admitted kind on a public relay) and `sharp-edges`.

| Attack | Result |
|---|---|
| Deleting someone else's video | A valid NFX deletion requires every `a` to be the signer's own 38504 address. `nfx-proto` refuses anything else, and so does the relay. Tested: a foreign deletion is `invalid:`, and `sign_deletion` refuses to sign one. |
| Deleting arbitrary events with an `e` tag | `e` tags are not allowed in an NFX deletion (vector case `e-tag`). |
| Using kind 5 to get general traffic onto a scoped relay | Only valid NFX deletions of served namespaces are admitted. They share the per-author budget with manifests: 12 an hour together (tested). |
| Replaying a deleted revision | The relay checks its stored deletions and refuses the replay (tested). Readers apply the deletion themselves anyway. |
| Relying on nostr-sdk's NIP-09 handling | It truncates NFX addresses, so the relay applies deletions itself at the full address, only up to the deletion's `created_at`. Readers never trust the relay to have applied one. |
| A relay that lost a manifest (restart) stopping seeders | Seeders stop only on an actual valid deletion (`Relays::deleted`), never because a manifest went missing. |
| A deletion hiding a later republish | NIP-09 timing applies: a revision newer than the deletion is current again (tested). |

Carried forward:
- **Delete-then-republish is visible.** A creator who deletes and then republishes a
  newer revision gets the video back, by NIP-09 design. This is documented, not a
  defect.
- **Deletions stay in the relay's store.** They count towards the 20,000-event cap like
  manifests.
- **Only "stop beaconing" is required of seeders.** Stored bytes remain (NFX-02 §6), and
  an origin that already holds the video keeps serving it by `root` until restarted.

Verdict: **no blocker.**

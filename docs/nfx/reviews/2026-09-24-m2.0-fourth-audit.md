# Fourth independent audit: M2.0 (`819cfee`, 2026-09-24)

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `5d16d80..819cfee`, branch `m2/contracts`: M2.0 and three reworks.
- **Method:**
  - 17 new mutants of the mock (13 survived, and one hung two scenarios instead of
    failing them);
  - exploit tests E1 to E20;
  - 41,377 parser cases against the Python reference;
  - the lock, read line by line. A safety classifier stopped the bypass runs, so the
    lock findings come from reading.
- **Controls:** 43/43 scenarios, 83/83 planted flaws and pay1 2/2 passed on the pristine
  tree.
- **sovtech's bar (2026-09-24): zero findings before the push.**
- **Verdict: not complete for push.**
  - **The honest viewer could be drained by a seeder that refuses on the transport:**
    200 chunks paid, 0 served.
  - A partial payment bypassed the global cap: 600 unpaid chunks at a cap of 10.
  - An honest pair broke on a fast reconnect.
  - Four viewer mutants lost 20 payments each.
  - Settling on a partial match passed.
  - The parser was clean: 0 disagreements.

The resolutions are in the commit that follows this record.

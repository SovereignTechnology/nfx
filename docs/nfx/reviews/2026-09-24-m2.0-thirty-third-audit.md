# Thirty-third independent audit: M2.0 (`f087126`, 2026-10-29), and round 34

- **Auditors:** four fresh auditors, one per lens, each from its own clone at `f087126`, with
  no author review to read: spec (the decisions as written: 2 Medium, 5 Low, 5 Info), crate
  (the money crate and the mock: 1 High, 1 Medium, 2 Low, 4 Info), teeth (the suite's teeth:
  8 Medium, 1 Low, 2 Info) and lock (the lock, CI and the documents: 1 Low, 11 Info). Their
  43 findings were merged into 35 points in six groups, and each group went to a refuter of
  its own, who tried to refute every point in it from a clone of its own and re-ran every
  planted defect in CI from the auditors' patches or its own.
- **Scope:** branch `m2/r33` at `f087126`, round 33's tip: the re-pin `cbcf6bd` and its
  documents.
- **Controls:** CI on `f087126` (run 40811478196): the lock job passed in 56 min 51 s of its
  120 minutes, the check job in 110 min 33 s of its 180.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready for `main`. No point was refuted outright: the 35 stand, some in
  part, and the refuters found 4 more. With sovtech's severity rule of 2026-10-29 (below)
  that is 3 High, 5 Medium, 8 Low and 23 Info. The honest mock holds every money bound the
  suite checks, exactly and attained (credit at most half the largest window quoted, rounded
  down). The three Highs are plausible watchers that break a bound without limit and pass
  all 152 scenarios of each harness file, the runner and every mutant: one that takes the
  whole of any file for the answer to a request a session's end carried over (41 sat for one
  file served over 40 sessions, where the bound is 2), one that gives up on a request 180 s
  after sending it (40 chunks paid, none served), and one that takes a new session's honest
  quote showing fewer chunks served than requested for the answer (40 paid over 40
  sessions).

**sovtech's decisions (2026-10-29).** Severity: a plausible planted defect that breaks a money
bound without limit and passes every scenario is High, as the thirty-second audit rated A1 to
A4. NS (course a): a request goes only on an open session; `requested` with none is
`Err(Unsent::NoSession)`. A-2: answers still belong to their session; a request that reaches
the seeder only after its session has closed is a stated concession. E-1: the cut-short
concession's text as written in NFX-07 §3a now; the decision of 2026-10-28 stands.

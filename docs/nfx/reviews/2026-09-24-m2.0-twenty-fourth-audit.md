The resolutions are in `4055978`.

**A `hello` behind payments.** NFX-07 §3 said a `hello` is answered once the payment in
progress is, at the latest at that payment's deadline, and that an account's payments
are processed in order of arrival. The mock hands a freed turn to whichever entry takes
it first, and a `hello` waits while any payment holds the turn. The spec now says that:
each payment holds the turn at most to its own deadline, a payment arriving meanwhile
may take it next, and payments take it in no set order. An honest watcher keeps one
payment in flight, so only a peer's own sessions queue behind one another. The new case
pins the rule it keeps: a `hello` never quotes while a payment holds the turn.

**When a waited `hello`'s wait ends.** At the last freeing of the turn before the `hello`
finds it free, which is what the mock takes and what lets `hello`s woken by one payment
share a read. NFX-07 §3 said "when it found the turn free" in two places, and a
spec-literal engine failed the suite. Both now match, and the claim promise is stated
from that freeing.

**Older-keyset proofs.** The mock's rotation after an expired active keyset also expired
the older keyset's proofs, so the honest watcher wrote off spendable ones. Older proofs
are now exempt from the rotated range, as they were from the active keyset's expiry.

**Runners.** The suite and `mutants.rs` share one runner, `adversary::run_on_a_thread`,
which records where a panic was raised. A planted defect counts as caught only if it
fails an assertion raised in `nfx-pay/src/adversary.rs`: a panic in the mock, the harness
or a runtime is refused as "panicked outside the suite". A thread a scenario spawns runs
its work under `adversary::keep_panic`, and `adversary::rejoin` raises its panic again on
the scenario's thread as from where it was first raised. `tests/runner.rs` checks the
runner itself, and the lock job requires its tests to run.

**Hooks a real engine's harness must reach.** `Harness::advanced_during_land` reports
whether the clock move asked for was made, and `racing_outcomes_settle_once` asserts it,
so a harness that ignores the request fails instead of passing untested.
`Harness::gather_admissions` says in its contract that the split-admission catch is
deterministic only where the harness reaches the point between check and count.

**Withdrawn from earlier records:**
- The twenty-third's "a takeover at the deadline writing no floor" is not equivalent (#2).
- The twenty-first's "a wait running through the deadline's own second (a payment cannot
  be in such a wait then)" is not harmless: `hello`s that passed the turn check before the
  payment took it can spend that second's reads (#3).

**Found while resolving:**
- Of the reads lens's mutants, `FreedFromOlderSecond` survived and was ported by mistake:
  its report had called it equivalent. It is not planted; it is listed above.
- `PayServedByAnyRead`, which that lens planted as a control, is caught at "two reads of A
  this second": the payment of other proofs reused the `hello`'s read. That is its own
  behaviour, and it is registered on both harnesses.
- The first port of the last-payment case failed on the honest mock: an earlier case
  left `fund_older_keyset` set. Each case now resets it.
- The first form of #21's runner judged a panic only by where it was raised on the
  scenario's own thread. `concurrent_admission_is_atomic` joined its threads with
  `expect("an admitting thread")`, so a panic in the engine on an admitting thread
  would have come back as a panic in `adversary.rs`, a catch. And the other threaded
  scenarios let `std::thread::scope` raise their threads' panics again from the standard
  library, so a suite assertion failing on a spawned thread would have been refused.
  No mutant relied on either. Both now go through `keep_panic` and `rejoin`, and no catch
  moved.

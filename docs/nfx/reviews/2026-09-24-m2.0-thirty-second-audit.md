# Thirty-second independent audit: M2.0 (`57a1c85`, 2026-10-25), and round 33

- **Auditors:** four fresh auditors, one per lens, each from its own clone at `57a1c85`, with
  no author review to read: spec (the decisions as written: 8 Medium, 5 Low, 5 Info), crate
  (the money crate and the mock: 2 Medium, 4 Low, 9 Info), teeth (the suite's teeth: 7 High,
  7 Medium, 2 Low, 2 Info) and lock (the lock, CI and the documents: 1 Medium, 4 Low, 6 Info).
  Their findings were merged into one list of five groups, and each group went to a refuter
  of its own, who tried to refute every finding in it from a clone of its own and re-ran
  every planted defect in CI from the auditors' clean patches.
- **Scope:** branch `m2/r32` at `57a1c85`, the thirty-second round's re-pinned tip.
- **Controls:** CI on `57a1c85` (run 40241168754): the lock job passed in 70 min 51 s of its
  90 minutes; the check job failed at `cargo deny` alone, crates.io having yanked
  crypto-bigint 0.7.6 to 0.7.8 that day, every test before it passing. #10 (crypto-bigint
  0.7.9, squashed to `main` as `cf9788a`) fixed it, merged into `m2/r32` as `a45c3eb`
  (run 40258422157: lock passed in 71 min, check in 106 min).
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready for `main`. 50 findings stand after refutation (the rows below):
  9 High, 11 Medium, 10 Low and 18 Info, and two unrated (A7, structural; N1-B, part of D3).
  Three were refuted (C11, C13, D7); a refuter's refutation of E9 was itself overturned. (The
  first count given to sovtech, "about 15 Medium, about 14 Low, about 20 Info", was an
  estimate; these are the rows.) No money bound the suite checks is broken by the honest
  mock. Eight Highs are defects the
  suite let through: a refusing seeder that takes one chunk more than half a window, or a
  seeder caught lying that is paid again. One High is a hole in the rule as written, which
  the honest watcher shows: an abandoned request stayed owed and freed its place, so 400
  abandoned requests paid 402 chunks for none served, and the watcher never stopped (probe
  40253527493).

**sovtech's decisions (2026-10-26).** S1 (a): abandoning answers nothing; a request is
answered only by its whole checked file or a `refuse` naming it on its session, and an
abandoned one keeps its place. S2 (a): the Harness doc follows the mock's whole outage (keys
and listings served). S3: D1's and D3's remaining calls accepted as written. S4: NFX-12's
per-session seeder close dropped, §5 pointing at NFX-07 §3a. S5: the lock job's limit raised
to 120 minutes.

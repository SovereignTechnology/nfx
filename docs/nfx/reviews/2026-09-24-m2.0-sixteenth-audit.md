# Sixteenth independent audit: M2.0 (`ea47d78`, 2026-09-25)

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `5d16d80..ea47d78`, branch `m2/contracts`. The fifteenth rework was examined
  hardest, against CDK 0.18.1's swap saga, keyset checks, error codes and signatory.
- **Method:**
  - 18 new seeder mutants, each run against all 62 scenarios with 9 controls: 5 caught,
    13 survived, 10 of them against the spec, each with a paired repro (four needed new
    harness hooks, added in the auditor's copy only);
  - three demonstrations on the honest mock;
  - 600,000 differential parser cases plus 14 by hand: 0 disagreements, 0 round-trip
    failures;
  - the lock job simulated on a clone, which passed in 77.6 s;
  - the check job simulated on a clone, with every variable `gitlab-ci.yml` sets, which
    passed;
  - 5 new lock routes in a disposable clone: all refused.
- **Controls:** 62/62 scenarios, 264/264 planted flaws and pay1 2/2.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready to push. 7 findings: 4 Medium and 3 Low, plus one cosmetic item.
  The fifteenth rework's 9 resolutions held, two of them only in part. The worst gap:
  with the mint's read endpoints hanging, a payment was answered 120 s after arriving,
  past its 60 s deadline, and an honest watcher gave up on an honest seeder.
- **Not findings:**
  - the trade-offs NFX-07 states, among them a quote up to a second stale (a `hello`
    reusing a read made that second) and a `hello`'s answer later by its own reads;
  - three harmless mutants: settling a completion's claim by restore (the same outcome,
    or learnt later), a split read that stops after an unanswered part (the rest is read
    at the next sweep), and a payment that reads after its checks, which the rework
    adopts.

The resolutions are in `97432a9`.

**A payment's reads, bounded in time and in number.** A `pay` now reads its account's
unknown swaps once, after its checks (structure, mint, DLEQ, amount, and its peer's ban)
and just before its swap. Those reads, and any completion, end at its deadline: one still
unanswered then is abandoned, and the payment is answered `mint-unavailable` at the
deadline. An account's own reads are at most two a second, whatever the proofs:
- a `hello` reuses any read of its account made that second;
- a payment reuses one made for the same proofs;
- past two reads that second, every entry reuses them.

**The request model, for retries too.** The in-deadline retry now goes through the mock
mint's request model, as the completion does. A new hook, `before_next_swap`, makes an
event happen just before the next swap request: the mint going down, restores stopping,
a keyset rotation after the seeder derived its outputs (stale keys), or the given-up
requests processed or starting.

**Cosmetic, fixed:** NFX-07's changelog entry for the fifteenth audit, and that audit's
resolution 7, said "§2" for the hello bound; it is in §3, identity and scope. (The
`77ff1f6` message says the same and stays, since commits are not rewritten.)

**Found while resolving:** the fifteenth rework's `ReadsBeforeBanCheck` became equivalent.
A payment's one read now comes after its checks, the ban among them, so no order of
the two is left to get wrong. The flaw and its separate early ban check are removed.

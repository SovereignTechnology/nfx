The resolutions are in `ec56c37`.

**Reads.**
- A `hello` that waited for a payment takes its floor as it finds the turn free
  (`Inner::wait_turn_at`). That is the reads sent by the last free before its wait ended,
  and a later free cannot move it.
- An entry works out the swaps to read, takes its place covering exactly them, and sends
  exactly them, all under the look's lock (`Inner::to_learn_in`, `Inner::learn_read_of`).
  An entry on another thread that looks meanwhile therefore waits for that read.

**The watcher after a 12003.**
- Each spent input is decided on its own.
- Whatever the spent inputs were, the unspent ones are decided per proof, and those not
  listed expired are taken back.
- An input pending, or a NUT-07 check unanswered, leaves the reclaim incomplete: the
  mock's reclaim now honours a state-check outage.
- An honest watcher never pays with proofs listed expired. With only such proofs, it
  pays nothing until the mint rotates.

**Keysets, as CDK has them.**
- `Harness::expire_active_keyset` expires every proof the keyset issued since the last
  rotation, those a wallet already holds included.
- `Harness::fund_older_keyset` gives the watchers proofs of an older keyset, which a
  wallet spends first.
- The mock refuses an already-expired input or output before reserving anything. A
  request reserved and then expired is `MintEvent::ExpireInputKeysetOnceReserved`.

**Runners.**
- Each scenario, in the suite and in `mutants.rs`, runs on a thread of its own and is
  waited for in real time. An engine that blocks its thread fails "the scenario hung"
  instead of hanging CI.
- A planted defect must now fail an assertion: a hang no longer counts as a catch.
- Both CI jobs have a `timeout:`.

**Deterministic meeting points.**
- `Harness::advance_during_next_land` moves the clock between an outcome's arrival and
  its settlement.
- `Harness::gather_admissions` makes admissions that check and count in two steps meet
  between them.

**Found while resolving:**
- The first form of the floor for #1 read it when the hello resumed, not when its wait
  ended. So did the twenty-second's, which #3 pins.
- Comparing where each of the 336 earlier defects is caught, before and after, showed
  that the new completion case, when first placed at the start of
  `bans_expire_and_state_stays_bounded`, caught `ExpiryDrops` and
  `UndecidedNeverExpires` ahead of their own section. It now sits just before the
  completion table.
- `AdmitSplitLock` was caught 19 times in 20 through thread timing alone, which would
  make the lock job fail at random. `gather_admissions` makes it 40 of 40.

## The rework, verified (author, before the twenty-fourth audit)

- **Suite:** 63 scenarios pass against the honest mock, and again with its reads as round
  trips. None were dropped. The honest threaded scenarios passed 40 runs out of 40 each.
- **Teeth:** 362 planted defects each fail an assertion, 26 of them new; none is caught by
  hanging. Each new one fails an assertion about its own behaviour.
- **No catch moved without cause.** Where each of the 336 earlier defects is caught was
  recorded before and after the rework. Six moved, each to its own behaviour:
  - `own_reads_per_proofs` and `payments_never_take_over`, from a hang to their own
    assertions;
  - `viewer_outputs_expiry_first` and `viewer_pays_with_expired_proofs`, to the rewritten
    keyset case;
  - `viewer_expired_by_code_alone` and `viewer_mixed_active_expired_writes_off`, to their
    "taken back" assertions: the honest watcher no longer pays during an expiry.
- **CI:** `crates/ci/check.sh` passes in full (adversary 63, round_trip 63, mutants 362,
  pay1 2).

**Correction** (from the [twenty-fourth audit](2026-09-24-m2.0-twenty-fourth-audit.md), #2):
"a takeover at the deadline writing no floor", listed above as equivalent, is not. A
`hello` that passed the turn check before the payment took the turn can read in the
deadline second, and without a floor that read served the `hello` that took the turn
over. It is now pinned (flaw `TakeoverWritesNoFloor`).

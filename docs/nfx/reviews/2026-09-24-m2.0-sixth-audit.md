- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `81a692e..4342b12`, branch `m2/contracts`. The fifth rework (`0da5c18..4342b12`)
  was examined hardest.
- **Method:**
  - 39 new mutants of the mock: 13 caught, 26 survived, 16 of those with measured
    impact;

## The rework, verified (author, before the seventh audit)

- **Suite:** 62 scenarios pass against the honest mock.
- **Teeth:** 154 planted defects are each caught. The 23 new ones were checked one by one.
  Two are caught by an earlier assertion: `ForgetsUnserved` also stops forgetting served
  accounts. One, `PayNoTakeover`, is caught by hanging.
- **Lock:** every route from all six audits was re-run and refused.

    workspace build.
  - An example or a binary compiling nfx-pay's files is refused. The first attempt
    exposed that uplifted artefacts were unchecked; fixed in `c898aed`.
  - An exported `env` function and a `BASH_ENV` script in CI are each refused, run
    directly or through `bash`.
  - The four `#[path]`/`include!` smuggles into nfx-node are caught by the check job's
    workspace mode.

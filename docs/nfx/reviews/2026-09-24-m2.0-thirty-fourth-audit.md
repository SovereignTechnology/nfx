# Thirty-fourth independent audit: M2.0 (`37e8e1f`, 2026-10-30), and round 35

- **Auditors:** four fresh auditors, one per lens, each from its own clone at `37e8e1f`, with
  no author review to read: spec (the round's text: 3 Medium, 4 Low, 13 Info), crate (the
  money crate and the mock: 4 High, 5 Info), teeth (the suite's teeth: 4 High, 3 Info) and
  lock (the lock, CI and the documents: 1 Medium, 5 Info). Their 42 findings were merged
  into 38 points in five groups, and each group went to a refuter of its own, who tried to
  refute every point in it from a fresh clone and re-ran every planted defect in CI, alone,
  from the auditors' patches or its own.
- **Scope:** branch `m2/r34` at `37e8e1f`, round 34's tip: the re-pin `da6e121` and its
  documents.
- **Severity scale:** sovtech's rule of 2026-10-29 included: a plausible planted defect that
  breaks a money bound without limit and passes every scenario is High.
- **Controls:** CI on `37e8e1f` (run 41143765468): the check job passed in 90 min 52 s of
  its 180 minutes. The lock job's first attempt was killed 4 min in by a runner shutdown
  (exit 137); its second passed in 75 min 36 s of its 120, every fact and count matching.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready for `main`. No point was refuted outright: the 38 stand, some in
  part, and the refuters found 6 more. With sovtech's ratings of 2026-10-31 (below) that is
  5 High, 5 Medium, 3 Low and 31 Info. The honest mock holds every money bound the suite
  checks, exactly and attained. The five Highs are plausible watchers that break a bound
  without limit and pass all 152 scenarios of each harness file, the runner and every
  mutant, each found by planting it in the honest mock:
  - one that gives up a request carried over a session's end, on some input of the next
    session that no hold check followed (a timeout, a `due`, a `last_pay`, an ack, a `rej`,
    a refused `hello`, a second quote, part of its file): 400 chunks over 400 sessions,
    where the bound is 2;
  - one that gives it up at the next session's end, or at the quote after two ends: 200
    over 400;
  - one that takes a request it has already paid for as answered, at an end, a quote, a
    `due` or a timeout: 400 over 400;
  - one that gives up a request 180 s or more after sending it on an input the suite never
    brought that late (part of its own file, an ack, a second idle timeout, any message of
    the seeder's, a `due` while the payment slot is free): 40 chunks paid, none served;
  - one that sends a retry of the unanswered request's own file, owed again: 41 paid, none
    served, in one session.

**sovtech's decisions (2026-10-31).** B-1 (course a): the standing records each video left
with a request unanswered, and no other identity asks that seeder for it meanwhile; the
cut-short concession drops "or under a new identity", which the decision of 2026-10-28 had
kept. B-2 (course a): an uploader that charges serves a peer that does not speak pay/1 as an
account that never pays, counted toward its `window` and the global cap. B-1 and E-1 are
rated Medium.

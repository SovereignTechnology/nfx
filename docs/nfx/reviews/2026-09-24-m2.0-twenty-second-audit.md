# Twenty-second independent audit: M2.0 (`779d101`, 2026-09-25)

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read. It was restarted once, when
  the session ran out of processes, and worked only from its own clones at `779d101`.
- **Scope:** `5d16d80..779d101`, branch `m2/contracts`. The twenty-first rework
  (`bf3f6b8..779d101`) was examined hardest: what a read covers, split reads in round
  trips, per-proof expiry, and the keyset margin.
- **Method:**
  - 12 new mutants (11 seeder, 1 viewer), 2 no-op controls, 2 fix probes and 12 existing
    flaws as controls, each run against all 62 scenarios on both harnesses: 3 caught, 9
    survived, 6 of them against the spec, with 16 paired repro tests. Every repro passes
    against the honest mock and fails under its mutant at its own assertion, never on a
    hang;
  - 600,000 differential parser cases over seeds 22001 to 22004, and 138 targeted cases:
    0 disagreements, 0 round-trip failures, 0 writer refusals;
  - the lock job on its own clone under `env -i`, with `CI=true` and `CARGO_HOME` inside
    the clone (passed in 1 min 35 s), and the check job with every variable
    `gitlab-ci.yml` sets (passed in 11 min 48 s);
  - 3 lock routes: tracked `json.py`, `hashlib.py` and the like beside `locked.py`
    (never imported, because of `-I`); a tracked `.cargo-home` symlink to a directory
    holding `config.toml` (refused); the same without the config (it supplies only the
    registry cache and index, verified and pinned as the CI cache is, so not a bypass).
- **Controls:** adversary 62/62, round_trip 62/62, mutants 319/319 and pay1 2/2.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready to push. 9 findings: 7 Low and 2 Info; no High or Medium, and
  no money bound breaks. The twenty-first audit's 7 resolutions held as implemented,
  though 2 of them were incomplete (#1 and #4 below). What the twenty-first found while
  resolving (reads count per account) held, caught by its own assertion. The correction
  appended to the twentieth record is accurate.
- **Not findings:**
  - three equivalent or harmless mutants: coverage by any undecided swap rather than all
    (an account never has more than one undecided swap), what an entry needs read fixed
    at its entry (the mock's recomputation on every poll is stricter), and a round trip's
    requests made when it comes back (a dropped read then never reaches the mint, which
    protects nothing);
  - the trade-offs NFX-07 states, among them the two-a-second bound counted per calendar
    second, a reused read that was sent and proved nothing, a payment refused
    `mint-unavailable` for want of a read, a first attempt refused 12003 or 12001 never
    being a ban, the expiry concessions and the cost of clock skew, and a reclaim held
    incomplete while the mint keeps an expired active keyset active;
  - a lying mint (a fake 12003 or listing): it already holds the watcher's funds.

The resolutions are in `7be5831`.

**Taking a read's place.** An entry now looks at its second's reads and, when it is to
read, takes its read's place in that same look, under one lock, before the read is sent
(`Inner::learn_own`'s plan, `Inner::take_place`). What the read covers is set as it is
sent, from what it asked the mint about. An entry at or past its deadline sends no read
and counts none.

**A hello that waited.** When a payment frees its account's turn, the second's read
record notes how many reads had been sent by then (`Inner::free_turn`). A `hello` that
waited for that payment is served only by a read sent after that point, so never by the
payment's own read.

**Expiry after a lost reclaim.** The mock's reclaim now says when the mint refused it
12003 (`MockNetwork::reclaim`). The watcher then restores its outputs, and when every
spent input is its own, it decides the unspent ones per proof. The per-proof reclaim
counts proofs its own earlier reclaim took back as back (`MockNetwork::reclaim_unexpired`).

**Found while resolving:** the first version of the F8 rule took each `hello`'s floor when
that hello resumed, not when its wait ended. Two hellos woken by the same payment then
could not share a read, the second waiting a second or reading again: within the bound,
but not "two entries at once share one read". Comparing, for all 319 existing flaws, the
assertion each is caught at before and after the rework showed it (`ReuseWithoutWaiting`
and `ReuseWaitsOnePoll` had moved). It also showed that the rework had merged two waits,
so `ReuseWaitsOnePoll` cut short a wait for the next second too. Both are fixed: the
floor is noted when the turn is freed, and the two waits are separate again.

## The rework, verified (author, before the twenty-third audit)

  trips. None were dropped; one is new.
- **Teeth:** 336 planted defects are each caught, 17 of them new, on both harnesses where
  they apply. Each new one fails an assertion about its own behaviour.

  now fails at "a read sent before B became unknown does not serve a hello after it",

  section.

  pay1 2).

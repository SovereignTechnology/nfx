**How this round was resolved.** The thirtieth round closed its findings cell by cell, and
this round's auditors planted their defects beside those cells: the auditor of the watcher
planted 112, of which 46 passed every scenario that builds a watcher and acted; the auditor
of the reads planted 26, of which 20 passed all 101 scenarios on four kinds of harness,
nine of them at a second quoted mint. So this round resolves by structure: a list is made
from the thing it enumerates and shared by the tables that need it, so that a member
missing from one table is missing from all and is seen once.

- *The watcher.* One list of a watcher's inputs is made from the `Viewer` trait
  (`VIEWER_METHODS` names its 13 methods, and a unit test reads them from the trait's own
  text), on the cell's own ledger, another video's and one made as the input comes, with a
  session open, ended or never held, and the clock alone over five spans up to a second
  past 30 days. One list of the states a standing can be in is crossed with it. Eight new
  scenarios run a rule's table over the two lists, two for a stop, two for a payment's
  place as time passes, one for the 180 s wait, one for a refusal's licence and two for the
  tries: 182,287 cells in all, each table pinning the number of cells it ran and skipped.
  In these tables the suite plays the seeder and judges the watcher by a model of what §3a
  has an honest watcher do (`World`); no engine runs in them. The older tables, which
  judge by a real seeder, stand beside them ("The reviews of what was removed").
- *The seeder.* A case pays at the mint its harness gives it (`Paying`). One list,
  `scenarios_at_every_quoted_mint!`, names the 39 scenarios in which a case pays a seeder
  itself, and each of them runs whole at a second quoted mint as well; every other scenario
  says why it runs at one mint in `AT_ONE_MINT`, and a test of the runner fails a scenario
  that is in neither list or in both. 39 tests more hold every answer of a mint that is
  judged at the first mint to be judged at the second, as many times. A defect that acts
  only at a seeder's other quoted mints has a registered form of its own
  (`catches_at_other_mints!`).
- *The turn and the lock.* Eleven unit tests pin what a turn of the suite's waiting is, and
  the lock refuses a file that uses the suite's macro without being named in
  `crates/ci/locked.py`.

   engine's or a harness's keeps making tasks of that runtime ready without pause: a
   thread wakes a task as an answer comes, and while several woke or started tasks with no

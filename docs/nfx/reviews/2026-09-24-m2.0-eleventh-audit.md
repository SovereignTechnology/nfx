# Eleventh independent audit: M2.0 (`d79bbf2`, 2026-09-25)

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `81a692e..d79bbf2`, branch `m2/contracts`. The tenth rework was examined
  hardest.
- **Method:**
  - 25 new mutants of the mock: 12 were caught, 11 survived with measured impact (each
    has a paired repro test), and 2 are equivalent. Two more repro tests failed against
    the honest mock itself (#1);
  - 580,300 differential parser cases: 0 disagreements, and 0 writer round-trip
    failures;
  - the lock job simulated on a pristine clone, which passed in 41 s;
  - the check job simulated on a pristine clone, with every variable `gitlab-ci.yml`
    sets and `CARGO_TERM_COLOR=always`, which passed in 7 min 15 s;
  - eight lock routes in disposable clones: all refused, and the stand-in shared cache
    was left intact.
- **Controls:** 62/62 scenarios, 207/207 planted flaws and pay1 2/2.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not complete for push. 9 findings: 2 High, 4 Medium and 3 Low, all in the
  tenth rework's new behaviour. Its 17 resolutions held; #3 and #4 only in part.
- **Not findings:** the trade-offs NFX-07 states, and two equivalent mutants:
  - settling on a quote that is then refused for price, window or mint: the loss stays
    at the one payment §3a concedes;
  - checking for an unknown swap before the key fetch rather than at the send, which is
    equivalent once #1 is resolved.

The resolutions are in `67ad8b9`.

**What counts as unknown.** The tenth rework counted only a lost answer as unknown. A swap
abandoned at the deadline while still in flight is unknown too: it may yet go through.
It now counts toward the one-per-account bound, and toward keeping the account. The
account's next payment is answered at once, without a swap, until the abandoned one's
outcome is known (NFX-07 §3). The suite's deadline scenarios changed to match:
- a payment queued behind a black-holed one now waits on held keys, not a held swap, so
  its deadline still counts from its arrival;
- a dead turn's taker is answered at once, and swaps once the dead swap has landed;
- a landing that must not release the next payment's turn now lands during that
  payment's key fetch.

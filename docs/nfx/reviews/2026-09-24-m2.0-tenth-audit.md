- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `81a692e..bcddf87`, branch `m2/contracts`. The ninth rework (the pay/1 wire's
  own crate, lost responses, retry and restore) was examined hardest.
- **Method:**

  - 451,482 differential parser cases: 0 disagreements;
  - the CI lock job simulated on a pristine clone, with a scratch `CARGO_HOME` copied

  - lock routes in disposable clones.

- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not complete for push. 17 findings: 1 High, 7 Medium, 5 Low and 4 Info. The

  suite did not require in every state (findings 5 and 6).
- **Not findings:**
  - designed trade-offs: one immediate retry rather than retries until the deadline;
    the mutant runner counting a hang as a catch; the lock's stated scope;
  - a retry's `spent` that a restore shows is another party's claim is never a ban
    (NFX-07 §3);
  - four equivalent mutants: a retry ignoring the deadline, a turn taken at the deadline
    and an outcome landing at exactly 60 s (both boundary conventions, safe either way),
    and banning after a genuine double-spend found on a retry.

The resolutions are in `7ce82cd`.

**A second look at the mock's retry and restore.** The ninth rework treated every
unanswered swap alike, and keyed a restore by the token. Both were wrong:
- **Two kinds of no answer.** A request that never reached the mint (it is down) has a
  known outcome: nothing happened. One that reached it and got no answer has an unknown
  one. Only the second must be learnt, so the mock now tells them apart (`Swap::Lost`),
  and NFX-07 §3 says so.
- **Restore by swap.** A restore now finds a swap's own outputs, derived for it alone
  (NUT-13) and reused by its retries, and no other swap's. The output sets come from the
  mint, unique across every seeder that shares it.
- **Bounded obligation.** At most one swap per account is ever of unknown outcome: while
  one is, the account's payments are not swapped (NFX-07 §3, bounded state).

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `5d16d80..a098cc6`, branch `m2/contracts`. The nineteenth rework was

- **Verdict:** not ready to push. 7 findings: 1 Medium, 4 Low and 2 Info. The nineteenth
  audit's eight resolutions and its four found-while-resolving items held. The worst:
  a watcher following §3a to the letter wrote off proofs it could still spend, on

- **Not findings:**

    keysets all expire within twice `account_ttl` gets no swaps; the rule by the seeder's

  - two consequences of stated rules: an account blocked until the completion at
    `account_ttl` when the mint's keyset expires under a reserved request; and the
    mock letting a keyset listed with no expiry expire anyway (a misbehaving mint);
  - one equivalent mutant: a reuse ignoring its deadline, since a reuse holds only
    within its second.

The resolutions are in `9b1ca54`.

**Reads of one second, each with a state.** The record of an account's reads now keeps
each read sent in the second: under way, back, or abandoned. An abandoned read still
counts, having been sent, but has no result. An entry:
- waits only for a read under way that would serve it;
- reads itself if the second's two reads are not spent, and otherwise in the next
  second;
- reads itself, as a read of the new second, when its read has not come back by the end
  of its second.

**The watcher's 12003.** A reclaim refused 12003 with its proofs unspent is lost to the
expiry only when the mint lists the proofs' own keyset as expired. Otherwise the expired
keyset is the reclaim's outputs', and the reclaim is incomplete and retried. The mock
models an expired active keyset kept active (`Harness::expire_active_keyset`) until
`rotate_keyset`.

**Found while resolving:** with the per-read record, a payment never waits for a read it
reuses. Payments are serialised per account, so while one payment's read is under way
the next waits for the turn, and after it the read is back. The nineteenth's
payment-waits-on-a-hello section had come to test the turn instead; it is replaced by
the second-end and budget cases above.

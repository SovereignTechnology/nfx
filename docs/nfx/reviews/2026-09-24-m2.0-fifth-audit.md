# Fifth independent audit: M2.0 (`00b1ac2`, 2026-09-24)

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `5d16d80..00b1ac2`, branch `m2/contracts`, with the fourth rework
  (`819cfee..00b1ac2`) examined hardest.
- **Method:**
  - 29 new mutants of the mock: 13 caught, 16 survived, 5 of those with money impact;
  - impact tests, including a threaded race of 20,000 runs;
  - 22,580 parser cases in two modes against the Python reference: 0 disagreements;
  - the lock, read line by line. A safety classifier stopped the setup of the bypass
    clone, so findings 12 to 15 and 18 come from reading.
- **Controls:** 52/52 scenarios, 102/102 planted flaws and pay1 2/2 passed on the pristine
  tree.
- **sovtech's bar: zero findings before the push.**
- **Verdict: not complete for push.**
  - The honest mock banned an honest peer after a dropped `pay`.
  - Under threads, it credited a payment it had just answered `mint-unavailable`.
  - The deadline takeover was untested.
  - The lock job ran unpinned code before its verdict.

The resolutions are in `c3145f8`.

# Twenty-fifth independent audit: M2.0 (`04bea39`, 2026-09-26)

- **Auditors:** four fresh agents, one per lens, working in parallel from their own clones
  at `04bea39`, with no author review to read:
  - reads: the seeder's own reads and the account's turn;
  - watcher: the watcher's reclaim and keyset expiry, against CDK 0.18.1's source;
  - teeth: the suite's teeth and the mock's fidelity, across all of `nfx-pay`;
  - lock: the lock, CI and the pay/1 wire.

  Each finding then went to a separate adversarial verifier, which tried to refute it
  from a clone of its own. All 22 were confirmed real and reproducible, and none was
  refuted or re-rated. The lock lens found nothing.
- **Scope:** `5d16d80..04bea39`, branch `m2/contracts`. The twenty-fourth rework
  (`0a70201..04bea39`) was examined hardest.
- **Method:**
  - reads: 11 mutants of its own and 2 references, each run against all 63 scenarios and
    6 repros on both harnesses (1,656 runs), with paired repros and probes of the spec;
  - watcher: CDK v0.18.1 cloned read-only and checked against every modelled mint and
    wallet behaviour; a planted flaw, two hooks and paired cases; a sequence explorer
    over one refused payment and the events around it;
  - teeth: 66 new mutants, each run against all 63 scenarios on both harnesses (8,316
    runs) through a runner that records where each fails;
  - lock: the lock job under `env -i` with `CI=true` (passed in 75 s), and the check job
    with every variable `gitlab-ci.yml` sets (every step passed); the re-pin reproduced
    exactly; new lock routes tried, none a bypass; the parser differential over new seeds
    and families, with 0 disagreements.
- **Controls:** adversary 63/63, round_trip 63/63, mutants 385/385, runner 4/4, pay1 2/2,
  on every clone before any mutant.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready to push. 22 findings: 2 Medium, 17 Low and 3 Info. No money bound
  is broken in the honest mock. The two Medium are suite gaps where a defect that breaks
  a money bound survived: a watcher accepting a short ack (#14), and a late "nothing"
  freeing its chunks from the global cap (#15). Of the 22:
  - 3 were NFX-07 text against the mock or itself (#1, #8, #11);
  - 1 was a runner that counted Rust's own runtime panics in the suite as catches (#20),
    and 1 a hook a lazy harness could satisfy (#21);
  - 2 were mock fidelity: an older keyset's proofs could not be held expired (#10), and
    third-party claims took proofs a mint holds reserved (#12);
  - 12 were gaps in the suite's teeth;
  - 3 were a doc comment, doc comments' case and a dead assertion (#9, #13, #22).

  The twenty-fourth audit's 22 resolutions held as implemented, apart from #2's reading
  of the takeover, withdrawn below.
- **Not findings:**
  - equivalent or harmless mutants: `FreedFromOlderSecond` (the twenty-fourth record's
    claim confirmed, 0 of 63 on both harnesses); a read that decides the holder's swap
    noting no floor (once that swap is decided the account has nothing left to read); a
    payment's send-time expiry abandon; a takeover not marking the holder abandoned;
    waiters not woken when a turn is abandoned or decided (each takes an expired turn over
    itself); the 180 s wait counted from the connection's close (a minimum); a re-ban
    keeping the first ban's time;
  - mock states CDK cannot reach with one active keyset per unit, which change no answer;
  - lock notes: the lock sees neither money logic re-implemented in another crate nor a
    re-pin by whoever can push (the concessions `check-locked.sh` states);
  - the trade-offs NFX-07 states.

The resolutions are in `47fd05d` to `c5a4cb8`. They were made in three groups on branches of their own,
each checked by an independent reviewer (who found 9 issues across the three, all
resolved before merging), then merged and verified together.

**When a turn held past its deadline is freed.** At the deadline, as NFX-07 §3 said
already, however late the payment's answer goes out, its swap's outcome comes, or an
entry takes the turn over. The mock noted the freeing only when something next touched the
turn, so what a `hello` behind it could reuse depended on poll order. Now a read sent in
the deadline's second or later (deadlines and the clock count whole seconds) is after the
freeing and serves the `hello` that waited, and a read sent earlier does not. NFX-07 §3 and
`session.rs` say so wherever they speak of the turn's end. `TakeoverWritesNoFloor` is the
rule now kept and is removed. The old rule is planted on every path at once
(`FreedAfterDeadline`) and on each path alone (`TakeoverFreedAfterDeadline`,
`ReleaseFreedAfterDeadline`, `AnswerFreedAfterDeadline`, `LandFreedAfterDeadline`), and
the deadline's second off by one either way (`DeadlineSecondLate`,
`DeadlineSecondEarly`).

**Reclaims into a keyset that expires sooner.** The watcher cannot apply the seeder's
margin, not knowing its `account_ttl`, and a reclaim held for a later keyset would pay
that seeder nothing for as long. So it reclaims to the mint's active keyset at once, and
NFX-07 §3a now says that this can move the value of an older keyset's proofs into one
that expires sooner, that the wallet must move proofs out of a keyset before its
`final_expiry`, and that the lost-answer concession holds whatever the proofs' own keyset.
Its old premise, "its proofs would be lost to the expiry anyway", is withdrawn.

**Claims the way a mint allows them.** `steal` and `steal_one` never take a proof the mint
holds reserved, lists expired or refuses as invalid, and `steal_one` names its proof (the
token's first unclaimed one). `Harness::reserve_rest` stands for a third party's request
that holds a token's unclaimed proofs reserved. `Harness::expire_older_keyset` expires an
older keyset whose proofs the wallets still hold, in one expiry state on every route.

**The runner.** `Ran::failed_the_suite` no longer counts a panic without a message, nor
a runtime panic Rust raises at a line of `adversary.rs` (overflow, division by zero, an
index out of bounds, a bare unwrap and the like), and the suite no longer subtracts engine
counts. The lock refuses `#[track_caller]` in every money-crate file but `adversary.rs`.
Its `nfx_pay` naming check now fails closed when `git grep` itself fails, as the teeth
lens's fixer noticed.

**Withdrawn from earlier records:**
- The twenty-fourth's #2: "that read serves no `hello` behind the takeover". The turn was
  freed at the deadline, so a read sent in the deadline's second or later serves it. Its
  flaw, `TakeoverWritesNoFloor`, is the rule now kept.
- The twenty-fourth's not-finding "`steal` ignoring reservations: no scenario relies on
  it". `a_pending_rest_keeps_the_reclaim_incomplete`, added in that rework, relied on
  `steal_one` doing so.
- The twenty-fourth's not-finding on "the takeover's second `free_turn` call" names code
  that is now removed.

**Found while resolving:**
- The first fix for #1 pinned only a holder that never sent its swap, and called the
  landing path equivalent; its reviewer showed a lost answer still leaves something to
  read. The in-flight case and the four path flaws came from that review, and so did
  the second before the deadline (`DeadlineSecondEarly`).
- `no_retry_at_the_deadline` leaves a mint event queued, which broke the swaps of cases
  placed after it; it runs last.
- The mock's older keyset could split into two expiry states through
  `expire_keyset_of`, `ExpireInputKeyset`, `ExpireInputKeysetOnceReserved`,
  `expire_keyset` and `MintEvent::ExpireKeyset`; every route now gives one state.
- The reviewers found gaps next to the findings, now pinned:
  `LastPayListingOnlyWhileActiveExpired`, `AckAcceptsPartial`, `AckAcceptsShortSpent`,
  every learner of a "nothing" (`OwnNothingFreesDebt` and the rest), `AdmitBanNotAged`,
  `BannedNotAged` and `BannedAdmitCountsToAccount`. The seeder's were each run against
  the whole suite at `04bea39` on both harnesses and passed it.
- Cargo reuses a stale test binary when copies of the crate at different paths share one
  target directory: gap probes need a target directory each.

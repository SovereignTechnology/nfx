# Thirtieth independent audit: M2.0 (`a444cd2`, 2026-10-11)

- **Auditors:** four fresh auditors, one per lens, working in parallel from their own clones
  at `a444cd2`, with no author review to read:
  - reads: the seeder's own reads, the account's turn and deadlines, a dropped video (7
    findings);
  - watcher: the watcher, §3a, §2's window rule and §4 (6);
  - teeth: the suite's teeth and the mock's fidelity (9);
  - lock: the lock, CI, the pay/1 wire and the removal of the demo's mirror (6).

  Each finding then went to a separate refuter, who tried to refute it from a clone of its
  own. Of the 28 findings 26 were confirmed and two were refuted (rows 23 and 24, both of
  the lock). One confirmed finding is another's again (row 16 is row 9's price finding), so
  25 are distinct. Three were re-rated down: row 15 from Medium to Low, rows 17 and 20 from
  Low to Info.
- **Scope:** branch `m2/audit-loop` at `a444cd2`, the tree the twenty-ninth record was
  committed on; the twenty-ninth rework examined hardest.
- **Controls:** CI on `a444cd2` (run 38155942160): the lock job passed in 42 min 49 s and
  the check job in 87 min 55 s.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready to push. 26 confirmed findings: 1 Medium, 15 Low and 10 Info. No
  money bound is broken in the honest mock. The Medium (row 14) is a gap of the suite: an
  engine that keeps a payment's proofs and credits nothing, at a quoted mint other than the
  first, passed all 101 scenarios. One Low (row 12) is of the spec itself: a bound NFX-07
  states that its own rules do not keep. It is **not fixed**: the choice is sovtech's
  ("Left open").

**How this round was resolved.** As in the twenty-ninth round, a finding is resolved for
its class where the suite runs the class: by a table or a case over its members, with the
planted defects registered against it. No new scenario was added: every case extends one
of the 101. The findings were resolved in three groups, each on a branch of its own from
`a444cd2`, then brought onto `m2/audit-loop` in this order:

- the lock, CI and the repository's documents (rows 19 and 23 to 28): `3acdcbf`, `3fa5907`,
  `63825e7`, `ebda656`, `bfaba7b`, `f145c46`;

  `ded7a2d`, `93b1aa4`, `d343819`, `ee6f536`, `9fb2037`, `2612136`, `9555691`, `3e13871`;

  `72e96e6`, `6df0c1b`, `cac269c`, `58a11ac`, `7a36ae3`, `d63bfd1`. One conflict met them
  on the way, the new entry of NFX-07's changelog, and both sides were kept.

- **Row 12, the half-window credit bound, is not fixed.** NFX-07 §3a's "credit on a video
  never grows beyond half its window, so a seeder that refuses everything takes at most
  half the watcher's ceiling for each video" is false once a watcher has two requests
  unanswered at a time. So are the sentences that rest on it: §3's concession for a dropped

  restatement per identity, NFX-10 §3's two sentences of a page, and the doc of the suite's

  window up where the rule rounds down. The true bound is floor(window / 2) + k − 1 chunks,

  at most half a window; the excess is paid for requests the watcher itself sent. The
  decision is sovtech's, between three courses, and none is taken yet:

  - count requests paid for and still unanswered as credit when paying ahead, and cap the
    requests unanswered at once at half a window: without the cap the bound is the larger

# Fourteenth independent audit: M2.0 (`20a7216`, 2026-09-25)

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `81a692e..20a7216`, branch `m2/contracts`. The thirteenth rework was
  examined hardest, against CDK 0.18.1's source.
- **Method:**
  - CDK 0.18.1's `check_state`, `restore`, swap saga, `add_proofs` and startup
    recovery, read from the thirteenth auditor's extracted sources;
  - 16 new mutants (15 seeder, 1 watcher), each run against all 62 scenarios with
    controls: 12 caught, 4 survived, each against the spec and with a paired repro;
  - demonstrations on the honest mock with harness additions: a per-request read
    limit, an unanswered read's cost in time, and a learner running during a read;
  - 600,000 differential parser cases from a grammar-aware generator: 0
    disagreements, and all 77,275 accepted cases passed both round trips;
  - the lock job simulated on a clone, which passed in 73 s;
  - the check job simulated on a clone, with every variable `gitlab-ci.yml` sets,
    which passed in 7 min 20 s;
  - 12 lock routes in a disposable clone: all refused.
- **Controls:** 62/62 scenarios, 246/246 planted flaws and pay1 2/2.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready to push. 8 findings: 1 High, 1 Medium and 6 Low. The thirteenth
  rework's logic held in the mock, and its resolutions held there. The gaps were where
  the mock was kinder than a real mint and engine: request size limits, synchronous
  entries that cannot make round trips, reads that take time to fail, and an expiry
  that could lose an away watcher's payment. Four were suite gaps.
- **Not findings:**
  - the trade-offs NFX-07 states: an account blocked while the mint holds its swap
    reserved (until the mint finishes or rolls it back), a lying seeder's one payment,
    the seeder trusting its mint's reads, the three tries a session;
  - a pending refusal never bans, so a token mixing spent and pending proofs (CDK names
    only the first) escapes a ban: bans deter, and it gains no service or credit.

The resolutions are in `29fed54`.

**When and how a seeder reads.** Two changes to NFX-07 §3 carry the rework:
- **Who reads what.** An account's own `hello` and `pay` read that account's unknown
  swaps, and no other account's. So a read that hangs costs a payment's deadline only
  on its own account. Admission and the other synchronous checks read nothing: they
  cannot make a mint round trip. A background sweep reads every account's swaps. The
  engine contract gains `SeederEngine::sweep`, which the suite calls where that time
  would pass.
- **Completion instead of expiry.** A swap whose inputs read unspent `account_ttl` after
  it became unknown is completed: the seeder sends it again, with the same outputs, and
  settles it by that answer. Its payer has had the inputs back all that time and never
  spent them, so the payment goes through after all and is credited late. An away
  watcher's next quote shows it, and a payer who parks undecided swaps pays for them.

**Found while resolving:** a harness flag leaked between sections of
`bans_expire_and_state_stays_bounded`. It set "lose the next response" twenty times with
one swap in flight, so the flag outlived the section and ate a later section's answer.
The section now loses the one response it has.

# Fifteenth independent audit: M2.0 (`23df03c`, 2026-09-25)

- **Auditor:** a fresh agent, with no author review to read.
- **Scope:** `5d16d80..23df03c`, branch `m2/contracts`. The fourteenth rework was
  examined hardest, against CDK 0.18.1's source.
- **Method:**
  - 24 new mutants, each run against all 62 scenarios with 6 controls: 10 caught, 14
    survived, 7 of them against the spec, each with a paired repro;
  - three demonstrations on the honest mock;
  - every one-shot harness flag instrumented across all 62 scenarios: none set twice
    unconsumed, none left set;
  - 600,000 differential parser cases: 0 disagreements, 0 round-trip failures;
  - the lock job simulated on a clone, which passed in 77 s;
  - the check job simulated on a clone, with every variable `gitlab-ci.yml` sets, which
    passed;
  - 10 new lock routes in a disposable clone: all refused, or not a bypass.
- **Controls:** 62/62 scenarios, 252/252 planted flaws and pay1 2/2.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready to push. 9 findings: 2 Medium and 7 Low. The fourteenth rework's
  logic and its resolutions held in the honest mock. The gaps:
  - the mock's completion sidestepped its own mint model, so its answer went untested;
  - a completion refused for good never ended its swap;
  - split reads, and the scoping of a hello's reads, were unpinned;
  - smaller spec, contract and cost gaps.
- **Not findings:**
  - the trade-offs NFX-07 or the lock's header states;
  - completed strangers kept as paying accounts, as any paying identity is;
  - seven harmless mutants: completing at `debt_ttl` or 60 s early (within the allowed
    configuration), completing on any undecided read (its own answer still decides),
    completing only in the sweep (the spec names no completer), a synchronous admit
    that reads its own swaps (impossible on the suite's runtime), a payment that reads
    once, and a sweep that skips when another runs;
  - one cosmetic item: NFX-07's changelog listed the last three audits out of order. It
    is fixed.

The resolutions are in `77ff1f6`.

**A completion is a retry.** The mock now sends a completion through its mint's request
model. Its answer can be lost, held, refused as pending or, after a keyset rotation,
refused for good; the new harness hooks can also process or reserve the original request
just before it arrives. NFX-07 §3 settles each case:
- answered `spent`, a restore of its outputs settles it;
- refused as pending, or unanswered, the swap stays unknown and is completed again later;
- refused for good (outputs of an inactive keyset, CDK 12002, or invalid inputs), a
  restore settles it too: signed is the first request's claim, and unsigned is nothing,
  since no request can sign those outputs any more.

| # | Sev | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Med | A completion bypassed the mock mint's request model, so its answer could never be lost, held or refused as pending, and three rules for that answer went unstated and untested. | As above. `bans_expire_and_state_stays_bounded`: a completion whose answer is lost is learnt by a later read; one answered `spent` after the original was processed just before it is settled by restore; one refused as pending after the original started just before it is credited once the mint finishes. Flaws `CompleteFreshOutputs`, `CompleteSpentIsNothing`, `CompletePendingIsNothing`. |
| 2 | Med | Which answer belongs to which swap after a split read was untested. | Same scenario: a claim and a request never processed, read one at a time, and each keeps its own answer. Flaw `SplitMisaligned`. |
| 3 | Low | Own-account scoping was pinned only for `pay`. | The honest hello's reads are counted, and a stranger's own other video pays in time and reads nothing. Flaws `HelloReadsAll`, `ScopeByPeer`. NFX-07 §3 names the peer's other videos. |
| 4 | Low | Splitting an unanswered read was untested. | NFX-07 §3: an unanswered read is not split. A sweep of 200 undecided swaps with restores unanswered makes 2 reads. Flaw `SplitOnUnanswered`. |
| 5 | Low | A completion the mint refuses for good (a rotated keyset, invalid inputs) never ended the swap. | Settled by restore, as above. `rotate_keyset` is a harness hook. With the keyset rotated, or the proofs forged, the swap ends and the account pays again. The retry path is settled the same way: a retry whose outputs are refused after a rotation, and whose first attempt went through, is credited. Flaws `CompleteRefusedStaysUnknown`, `CompleteInvalidStaysUnknown`, `RetryRefusedIsKnown`. |
| 6 | Low | NFX-07 named CDK 11014 for an oversized read; an oversized restore is 11015. | NFX-07, the harness docs and the mock name both. |
| 7 | Low | A hello's own reads broke its promised bound (answered by the payment's deadline). | NFX-07 §3 (identity and scope) and the contract: the bound plus the time its own reads take. |
| 8 | Low | Own-account reads had no rate bound, and a payment's reads came before its ban check. | At most one a second per account: a `hello` reuses a read of its account made that second, and so does a `pay` of the same proofs. A watcher paying again after its reclaim pays with other proofs, and reads afresh. A `pay` reads nothing before its ban check. 100 hellos cost one read, and so do 100 payments of one token; a banned peer's payment reads nothing. Flaws `OwnReadsUnbounded`, `ReadsBeforeBanCheck`. |
| 9 | Low | The `sweep` contract left two constraints unstated. | `SeederEngine::sweep` states them: the engine under test sweeps only when called, and a sweep that finds another running returns or runs alongside, never waits. |

**Found while resolving:** the per-second bound changed three scenarios, each reading
again within the same second on the harness's one-second clock. A watcher that paid
again resending its refused token now pays with fresh proofs, as §3a has it. Two quotes
taken right after the mint recovers now come a second later.

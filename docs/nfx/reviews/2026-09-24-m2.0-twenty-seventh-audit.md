# Twenty-seventh independent audit: M2.0 (`5b4f530`, 2026-09-27)

- **Auditors:** four fresh agents, one per lens, working in parallel from their own clones
  at `5b4f530`, with no author review to read:
  - reads: the seeder's own reads, the account's turn and deadlines;
  - watcher: the watcher and §4, against CDK 0.18.1's source;
  - teeth: the suite's teeth and the mock's fidelity, across all of `nfx-pay`;
  - lock: the lock, CI and the pay/1 wire.

  Each finding then went to a separate adversarial verifier, which tried to refute it
  from a clone of its own. All 15 were confirmed real and reproducible; none was refuted,
  and one was re-rated up (#9, Low to Medium).
- **Scope:** `5d16d80..5b4f530`, branch `m2/contracts`. The twenty-sixth rework
  (`caf4011..5b4f530`) was examined hardest, and the seams where its groups were merged.
- **Controls:** adversary 63/63, round_trip 63/63, mutants 603/603, runner 8/8, pay1 2/2,
  on every clone before any mutant.
- **sovtech's bar:** zero findings.
- **Verdict:** not ready to push. 15 findings: 3 Medium, 10 Low and 2 Info. No money bound
  is broken in the honest mock. Two Mediums are suite gaps on the watcher (#7, #8); the
  third is the lock (#14): a build directory restored with the lock job's cache made it
  pass a commit whose money tests fail.
- **Not findings:** equivalent mutants (each recorded with its reason by the group that
  planted it, below); a ban checked a second time just before the swap, and a `hello`
  counted toward the cap through its reads, which NFX-07 leaves open; the trade-offs
  NFX-07 states.

The resolutions are in `641b8a9` to `f3f442c`. They were made in three groups on branches of
their own (a: the seeder, b: the watcher and §4, c: the lock and the runner), each
checked by an independent reviewer and corrected, then merged. Each group also hunted its
own part of the mock with mutants of its own, and pinned what survived (below).

**The seeder.** A payment whose turn comes in its deadline's second or later runs none of
its checks, the ban's included, and is answered `mint-unavailable`. A `pay` dropped before
its swap is sent, at any stage, leaves no account, and frees the turn then only if it
held it, waking what waits for it; so does any refusal. A `hello` refused as it answers,
`banned` or `bad-session`, on whatever path it came to answer (after a wait, after taking
a dead turn over, or after waiting for nothing), leaves nothing, and a `hello` dropped
before it is answered keeps nothing. NFX-07 §3 and `session.rs` say so. The honest mock
already behaved so: the gaps were in the suite.

**The watcher.** Nothing but a quote that settles it, or the reclaim, settles what the
watcher waits on: a refused quote (price, window, mints), a second quote, a refused
request or `hello`, the session's end, a timeout, and another video's ledger or quote
leave the payment in flight, a payment awaiting a quote and an incomplete reclaim as they
were, and undo no stop. A settling quote must match `accepted_upto` as well as
`spent_total`. The watcher reclaims whether or not it holds a session, and a reclaim done
with no session, or from another video's ledger, ends as one done in a session on the
payment's own; the mock's `due` now does so, as `Viewer::due` always said. Whatever
stopped it, a stopped watcher still reclaims. A closed session's payment is reclaimed
180 s after it was sent, whatever came since.

**§4, origins.** A refusal is any answer but `200` to a paid request, and an unanswered
one is reclaimed 180 s after sending; the client keeps one paid request in flight to an
origin. A reclaim that finds a proof spent loses that payment, and the client pays that
origin nothing more, so a lying origin takes one payment. With every proof back it may
pay again. A `503`, or no answer, with every proof back uses no try, since the mint may be
down: the client pays again later, backing off. After three paid requests in a row
refused otherwise (a `402`, or another status), it stops paying that origin; only a `200`
resets the count. The fix group first counted `503`s too, which would have dropped an
honest origin for good through a mint outage, since an origin has no session to restore
its tries; the author took them out at the merge. §4 is text only: M2.0 has no origin
code.

**The lock and the runner.** `lock-job.sh` refuses any untracked or ignored path in the
checkout but the restored registry cache and index, and `locked.py` builds and runs the
money tests in a target directory made for the run, refusing in CI any artifact reported
reused. `gitlab-ci.yml` no longer claims the cache isolates pipelines. A runner test reads
`adversary.rs`'s code as the line rule does and fails on `unwrap` or `unwrap_err` in code,
so the function-value form fails CI as the call form does.

**Decisions taken at the merge:**
- §4's three tries exclude a `503` or no answer with every proof back, as above.
- §3a's wait is "180 s after sending it, whatever came since", the stricter reading; the
  suite already pinned "at 180 s, not at 179 s".
- §4 keeps one paid request in flight to an origin; without it, "a lying origin takes one
  payment" would be false.

**Found while resolving:**
- The first fix for #4 dropped the payment after its keys came; its reviewer showed an
  honest engine may send the swap as they come, so the case now drops it with its keys
  held.
- Group b's first pass covered refused quotes on the waiting ledger only, and #10 for one
  outcome only; its reviewer found both, and both are pinned.
- Probes deadlocked twice on their own lock handling (a guard held across a read); those
  were probe bugs, not findings.
- Catches moved while the groups worked; each was restored by ordering the new cases,
  never by rewording a message.

**Corrections to the twenty-sixth's record:**
- Its "Removed" paragraph: `UnsentCreatesAccount` was not equivalent. "A send that finds
  its deadline passed follows a read of the account's own swap, so the account exists" is
  false: an unknown swap creates no account. Merge `a6d330c`'s message says the same. It
  is restored (#1).
- Its hunt line "every way of undoing a stop" was not pinned: no refused or second quote
  reached a stopped watcher (#8).
- Its not-finding "a stale target directory defeating the lock locally, which CI's clean
  checkouts exclude" was false: the job's cache restores a build directory into the clean
  checkout (#14).
- "`clippy::unwrap_used` is denied, so a new one fails CI" held for the call form only
  (#15).

**Left open:**
- Some flaws are caught on round trips only, where a read is under way after one poll;
  on the synchronous harness no read yields, so no id or ban can change during one.
- Some set-ups hold a payment's keys after the seeder has fetched them for another; an
  engine that caches keys fails their set-up assertion, never passes wrongly.
- The viewer's flaws run on one harness: `mutants.rs` has no round-trip viewer harness.
  The groups checked both in scratch probes.

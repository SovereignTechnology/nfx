## Fixes applied before the push

Beyond the table above:
- **S1:** `ContentStore` implementers now supply `raw_read`, `raw_write` and `remove`
  only. The provided `get`, `put_verified` and `put` do the hashing, so a new backend
  cannot skip it.
- **S2:** `Relays::manifests` and `watch_beacons` fail when no relay is connected
  (tested).
- **S3:** `--https-url` is validated at start, the same way readers validate it, and
  a beacon that reaches no relay shows as `Unannounced(reason)` rather than `Seeding`
  (tested both ways).
- **Behaviour change:** a fetch now stores each member as soon as it verifies. On a
  lie, earlier verified members stay; they are the right bytes. The lying member is
  never stored.

A real-relay subtlety found while testing #4: a stock relay keeps only the newest
revision per address. A creator who future-dates a revision therefore hides the video
from readers (who ignore it) until that time, rather than taking it over. It is
self-inflicted only, since only the author's key can sign, and scoped relays refuse
the event.
